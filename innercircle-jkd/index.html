First-class questions

Starting should feel simple.

Do I need martial arts experience?

No. The beginner path is designed for people starting from zero, and training can be adjusted to your current ability.

Do I need to be in shape first?

No. Fitness develops alongside technique. The goal is a repeatable training habit, not an audition.

Is private training available?

Yes. Coach Mike offers individualized private personal protection training, and a recurring private session is included with the Elite membership.

Where is training held?

Private and academy training is held at 4037 Dixie Highway, Elsmere, KY 41018. Group and corporate sessions can also be arranged at an agreed location.
